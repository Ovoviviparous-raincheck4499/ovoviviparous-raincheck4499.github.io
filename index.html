🔧 First-Time Setup Guide

Getting your iPhone and PC connected takes just a few moments.

Option 1: USB Connection (Recommended)

  • Plug your iPhone into your computer using your charging cable.
  • When your iPhone asks "Trust This Computer?", tap "Trust".
  • Open omarchy-iphone-mirror on your PC. Your screen should appear right away.

Option 2: Wi-Fi Connection

  • Make sure your iPhone and PC are on the same Wi-Fi network.
  • Open omarchy-iphone-mirror on your PC.
  • The app will automatically find your iPhone. If not, check that both devices are on the same network.

⚠️ Note: For Wi-Fi, your iPhone must be unlocked during the session. For USB, keep the cable securely connected.

🛠️ Common Troubleshooting

  • No connection? Unplug the USB cable, restart both devices, and try again. For Wi-Fi, restart your router.
  • Screen is blank? Make sure your iPhone is unlocked and the screen is on.
  • App won't open? Check that Windows didn't block the download. Look in your Downloads folder and right-click the file, then select "Run as administrator".
  • Laggy mirroring? Switch to a USB cable instead of Wi-Fi for a faster connection.
